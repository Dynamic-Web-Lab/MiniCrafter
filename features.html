MiniCrafter

A free, kid-safe block-building game that runs right in your browser. Build worlds, make your own games, learn to code with a robot and play together, with no ads, no install and no chat with strangers.

Why MiniCrafter is different

  • Nothing to install, nothing to buy. It runs in any browser, on computers, Chromebooks, tablets and phones, and keeps working offline.
  • Safe by design. No ads, no tracking and no text chat. Friends play together with emoji only, and the host lets each player in.
  • Build games, not just worlds. The Course Maker turns your builds into timed obstacle courses with coins and checkpoints.
  • Learning built in. Program Bit the robot with blocks, and finish quests that practise counting, times tables, colours and shapes.
  • Grows with you. Gentle modes for young children, and an optional Adventure mode with monsters and combat for teens and grown-ups.

All features

🏁 Course Maker: build your own games

Place a Start pad, Checkpoints and a Finish pad, scatter coins, then race the clock. Fall off and you return to your last checkpoint. Finishing celebrates with confetti and saves your best time. Mix in trampolines, speed strips, ice, fans and portals for wild obstacle courses.

👫 Play Together, safely

Up to 4 players build in one world with a room code. Players pick names like "Blue Fox", send emoji instead of chatting, and the host lets each player in. Animals, day and night and circuits are shared. (Online edition, with a free account.)

🤖 Learn to code with Bit

Drag Blockly blocks to make Bit move, build, dig, wait and flip circuit levers, using loops, if/else and sensors. Starter programs to remix, and a "see the code" view.

📷 Photo Mode

Snap your builds, then add a frame, a filter, stickers and a caption, and save or share the picture. Photos stay on your device.

🐶 Friendly animals and pets

Pat, feed and shear animals, watch babies grow, and tame a dog or cat as your pet. Pets get name tags and can wear party hats, crowns and sunglasses. Later on, give them pet armor in three tiers, a backpack that holds 9 stacks of your things, and a pet bed or doghouse where they rest and get better. Frogs, butterflies and fireflies appear around your builds.

🌾 Farming and gardens

Plant flowers, water your garden, grow wheat and bake bread. A watering can makes things bloom.

🎉 Fun blocks

Trampolines, speed strips, slippery ice, fan vents, glowing neon, music stones you can play by walking, confetti pads, colourful portals and a Hidden Star to hide for someone to find.

🏘️ Villages and trading

Visit villages with named farmers, builders and smiths, and trade with them.

⛏️ Creative and gentle Survival

Build freely with unlimited blocks and power tools, or collect, craft tools and look after your hearts in a gentle Survival mode with no monsters.

⭐ Quests that teach

64 quests with stars guide new players and practise counting, adding, times tables, colours and shapes, plus a coding track.

📱 Tablets, phones and Chromebooks

Touch controls with a joystick, compact layouts for phones, install it as an app, and play offline.

⚡ Spark circuits

Redstone-style wires, levers, buttons, pressure plates, batteries, inverters, delays, lamps, doors and pistons. Build blinking lights, secret doors and piston elevators, and let Bit flip the levers.

🪄 Power tools

Bridge wand, box builder, house walls, dome maker, paint roller and rainbow brush, with undo and redo.

⚔️ Adventure mode (teens and grown-ups)

Behind a grown-up check: hunger, XP and levels, cartoon night creatures, swords, bows, shields, armour, a Lost Items Chest and an optional Hardcore mode.

🌍 Endless worlds

Procedural biomes, caves, lakes, day and night with torch lighting, several saved worlds, world files and share links.

Frequently asked questions

Is MiniCrafter free?

Yes. MiniCrafter is completely free: no ads, no in-game purchases and no subscriptions. It is open source under the MIT License.

Is MiniCrafter safe for kids?

Yes. There is no text chat, no ads and no tracking. In Play Together, players only see picked names like "Blue Fox" and talk with emoji, and the host lets each player in. Accounts need only a made-up username and password, and players under 13 need a grown-up to help.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. MiniCrafter runs in any modern web browser on computers, Chromebooks, tablets and phones. You can also add it to your home screen like an app, and it keeps working offline.

Does MiniCrafter need an account?

No. You can play without an account. An optional account, with just a made-up username and a password and no email, unlocks Play Together.

Can I play MiniCrafter with friends?

Yes. Play Together lets up to 4 players build in the host's world with a room code. Players need an account, talk with emoji only, and connect through a safe relay rather than directly to each other.

Does MiniCrafter work on iPad, Chromebook and phones?

Yes. It has touch controls with a joystick, works on iPad, Android tablets, phones and school Chromebooks, and adjusts its layout for small screens.

How is MiniCrafter different from Minecraft?

MiniCrafter is free and runs in the browser with no install. It is designed for children first, with no chat with strangers, built-in learning quests, a codable robot called Bit, a Course Maker for building your own obstacle-course games, and a Photo Mode. It is an original game, not affiliated with Minecraft or Mojang.

What age is MiniCrafter for?

Creative and gentle Survival modes suit children from about 6. Teens and grown-ups can switch on Adventure mode, behind a grown-up check, for monsters, combat, hunger and Hardcore.

Can kids learn to code in MiniCrafter?

Yes. Kids program Bit the robot with drag-and-drop Blockly blocks (loops, conditions and sensors) to build, dig and flip circuit levers, and can view the matching JavaScript-style code.

Can teachers use MiniCrafter in class?

Yes. It needs no installs or student accounts, works offline on school Chromebooks, and its quests practise counting, times tables, colours, shapes and coding.
