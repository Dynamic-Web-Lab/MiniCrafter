🛡️ Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-10 · Last updated 10 November 2026

🧒 The short version (for kids)

  • We don't know who you are. We never ask for your real name, email, address, phone number, school or photos.
  • Your worlds stay on your device unless you choose to play online.
  • If you make an account, we keep only a made-up username and your password (scrambled so nobody can read it). Never use your real name as your username!
  • No ads, no tracking, nothing to buy. We never sell anything about you.
  • In Play Together other players only see a colour-and-animal name like "Blue Fox". There's no chat, just emoji.
  • You can delete your account any time, and it's gone straight away.
  • Under 13? Ask a grown-up to help you make an account.

Questions or requests about privacy: info@dynamicweblab.com.

2. Two ways to play

  • Free edition and playing without an account: everything happens in your browser. We receive nothing about you or your game.
  • Online edition with an account: adds features that need our server, such as Play Together. Only then do we keep the small amount of information below.

3. What is stored on your device

The game saves these in your browser's storage on your device, so it can work offline. We can't see them.

  • Your worlds, quest stars, settings and hotbar.
  • Your Play Together colour-and-animal name.
  • For Adventure mode, a note that the grown-up check was passed, which is cleared when the browser is closed.
  • A copy of the game's files, so it works without internet.

You can remove all of this by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

5. Play Together

  • Players connect through our server, never directly to each other, so your internet address isn't shared with other players.
  • Rooms exist only while the game is running. We don't record or store what happens in a room.
  • Other players see only your colour-and-animal name, your character and the emoji you send. Your username stays private.
  • The host lets each player in and can remove players.

6. Keeping things safe

  • To stop people guessing passwords or flooding the service, our server briefly remembers internet addresses in memory only, for up to one hour. They are never written to disk.
  • We switch off request logs for the game. Technical error logs may briefly contain an internet address while we fix a problem.
  • Our account database is backed up every night in encrypted cloud storage. Backups are kept for up to 30 days, so a deleted account fully disappears from backups within 30 days.

7. Services that help us run MiniCrafter

We don't sell or share your information for advertising, ever. These providers process data only to run the service for us:

  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it from attacks. It handles internet addresses and may set a short-lived security cookie.
  • Our hosting provider runs the servers where the game and accounts live.
  • Microsoft Azure stores our encrypted nightly backups.
  • GitHub hosts the free edition, if you play that version.

These providers may be located outside your country. We use them with protections required by law.

8. Children

  • MiniCrafter is made for children, so we collect as little as possible and never use anything for advertising or profiling.
  • Players under 13 can make an account only with a parent or guardian, who confirms when the account is made.
  • Grown-ups can ask us to see or delete a child's account at info@dynamicweblab.com. Please include the username.

9. Your rights

  • Delete: open the 👤 account screen in the game, choose "Delete my account" and enter your password. Your account, sessions and agreement record are deleted straight away.
  • See, correct or ask questions: email info@dynamicweblab.com. Depending on where you live (for example the UAE, the EU, the UK or the US), you may have more rights. We'll help with all of them.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll show a notice in the game before it applies.
